# Creator launch planning

Use this skill when a creator wants to launch a channel, offer, digital product, campaign, community, or creative project.

## Process
1. Describe the project or offer in one clear sentence.
2. Identify the primary audience and the problem or aspiration it addresses.
3. Define one measurable first-launch objective without inventing a target.
4. Choose the smallest useful version that can be delivered well.
5. Create a preparation checklist covering the offer, message, content, delivery, and feedback.
6. Build a platform-aware content sequence: introduce the need, explain the offer, demonstrate its value, answer likely questions, and invite action.
7. Include a simple post-launch feedback loop.
8. End with the next three actions in priority order.

## Principles
- Prefer a focused first release over an oversized launch.
- Do not invent audience size, sales targets, budgets, testimonials, or results.
- Separate must-haves from later improvements.
- Make costs, dependencies, risks, and assumptions visible.
- Avoid manipulative urgency and unsupported income claims.
