# Repeatable Production Workflow

## 1. Intake
Capture objective, audience, channel, deliverables, deadline, CTA, brand kit, references, and approval owner.

## 2. Plan
Choose an approved template family. Create a content brief, script or copy, storyboard if video, and asset list.

## 3. Produce
Duplicate the master into a project-specific working copy. Customize brand tokens and content. Keep source assets separate from exports.

## 4. Review
Check accuracy, brand fit, spelling, accessibility, rights, dimensions, captions, and CTA. Obtain approval before publishing or delivery.

## 5. Export and deliver
Use consistent filenames. Deliver agreed formats and editable sources. Include a short usage note.

## 6. Archive and improve
Update the asset register, record version and rights, archive final files, and capture reusable improvements without retaining confidential client information.

## Definition of done
A deliverable is complete only when the approved source, final export, rights record, QA checklist, and asset-register entry are present.
