# Productized Content Packages

Packages are starting scopes, not fixed promises. Confirm client needs, rights, revisions, and delivery dates in a written proposal.

## Starter Content Kit
- 6 social graphic templates
- 2 story layouts
- 1 caption / CTA worksheet
- Brand-token setup
- Editable source and export files

## Creator Growth Kit
- 10 social graphic templates
- 3 carousel systems
- 3 short-video scripts and storyboards
- 3 thumbnail / cover layouts
- Caption and publishing checklist

## Brand Content System
- Brand kit and usage guide
- 15 reusable graphics / layouts
- 5 video formats with scripts and storyboard templates
- Export presets and content calendar starter
- Handover guide and recorded walkthrough outline

## Client delivery requirements
- Approved brief and brand assets.
- Written scope, timeline, and revision limit.
- License and usage rights documented.
- Final exports plus agreed editable files.
- Client sign-off and archive.
- Remove or anonymize confidential client data before reusing any generic learning or template.
