# Reusable Video Production System

## Master formats
- Vertical short: 9:16, 15–60 seconds.
- Square cut: 1:1, where a platform or campaign requires it.
- YouTube / presentation: 16:9.
- Always confirm platform specifications before final export.

## Repeatable video formats
### A. Brand introduction (20–30 sec)
1. 0–3s: visual hook and brand promise.
2. 3–10s: who the brand serves and the problem it addresses.
3. 10–20s: value, proof, or example.
4. 20–30s: clear CTA and branded end frame.

### B. Educational tip (20–45 sec)
1. Hook: name the audience problem.
2. Teach: one useful idea in 2–4 steps.
3. Demonstrate: example or quick walkthrough.
4. Close: one action and CTA.

### C. Story / founder update (30–60 sec)
1. Context: what is happening.
2. Tension: obstacle or lesson.
3. Progress: what changed or what was learned.
4. Reflection: takeaway.
5. CTA: invite a response or next step.

## Required production documents
- Script with spoken words and on-screen text separated.
- Storyboard with scene number, duration, shot, visual, narration, and transition.
- Shot list / asset list.
- Music and footage rights record.
- Caption file or reviewed subtitles.
- Export settings and final review checklist.

## Editing and delivery checklist
- First 2 seconds communicate a reason to keep watching.
- Audio is clear and properly licensed.
- Captions are accurate and readable.
- No unintended black frames, clipping, or spelling errors.
- CTA and brand end card are present where appropriate.
- Export aspect ratio, resolution, frame rate, and file size checked.
- Keep project file and final master; create platform variants from the master.
